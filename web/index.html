What runs where on Tari

No server of ours holds the payment record. Each step uses a piece of the Tari Ootle stack.

  1. Payer key · intent sign

    Sign the job once

    The payer signs recipient, amount, expiry and job ID. Ootle’s signature binding ties it to mainnet and to this one vault component, under the domain semvi.payout.v1.

  2. Any wallet · tari_ootle_walletd

    Relay it, again and again

    A relayer builds a fresh transaction with Tari’s transaction builder and pays the network fee. Retries can use another account. The relayer cannot change who gets paid.

  3. Validators · Semvi template (WASM)

    Pay and record in one step

    // src/lib.rs · inside execute()
assert!(!self.consumed.contains(&job), "OperationConsumed");
// …expiry and signature checks…
self.consumed.insert(job);
let payment = self.funds.withdraw(amount);
ComponentManager::get(recipient).invoke("deposit", args![payment]);

    If the deposit fails, the whole step rolls back and the job stays unpaid.

  4. Public indexer · indexer-a.ootle.tari.com

    Anyone can check

    Receipts, the vault’s job list and its balance are public substates. This page reads them directly, with no backend.

Operator console

Drive a real payout and retry through your own Tari wallet daemon. The console runs on your machine, so it only works when this page is served by node console/server.mjs.

cargo build --examples
TARI_WALLET_COOKIE_FILE=~/.tari/mainnet/.cookie \
SEMVI_PAYER_KEY=/secure/payer.key \
SEMVI_VAULT=component_… SEMVI_RECIPIENT=component_… \
SEMVI_RELAYERS=relayer-a,relayer-b SEMVI_APPROVED_NETWORK=mainnet \
node console/server.mjs

Then open http://127.0.0.1:4317/#console. Without SEMVI_APPROVED_NETWORK the console signs but will not submit.

Calls to Tari

    What it promises, and what it doesn’t

    • At most once per vault and job ID. Not guaranteed delivery. If a payment never lands, the job stays unpaid and can be sent again.
    • 256 job IDs per vault, kept forever. A full vault refuses new jobs; the payer can still withdraw. A new vault starts a new card.
    • A refused retry still pays its network fee, about 0.002 TARI on mainnet today.
    • Everything is public. Amounts, recipients and job IDs are visible on chain. Hash business references you don’t want to reveal.
    • Signed jobs can’t be revoked early. Keep expiry short. Refilling a vault can make an unexpired job payable.
    • Our mainnet runs use accounts in one demo wallet. No outside users or audit are claimed yet.

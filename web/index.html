Why this is different from replay protection

Every attempt has a different transaction hash. The second relayer submits a new transaction for the same business job. Semvi refuses it because the job is consumed—not because a transaction was replayed.

Read the contract’s boundaries

At most one payment per job ID, per vault. This does not guarantee delivery. Consumed IDs are never pruned; each vault holds up to 256 jobs. A new vault has its own namespace. Payment amounts and recipients are public. A refused retry may still cost network fees.

Sign once, relay again

Create a dedicated intent key, sign a job for a fixed vault and recipient, and build a fresh transaction for every retry. Amounts are in microTARI.

cargo build --examples
target/debug/examples/intent keygen /secure/payer.key
target/debug/examples/intent sign /secure/payer.key \
  mainnet VAULT JOB_HASH RECIPIENT AMOUNT EXPIRY
target/debug/examples/prepare request-spec.json

Replace the uppercase arguments with your values. A vault must already exist and be funded. Preparation and signing do not submit anything.

Dry-run before spending
